Fishing Expansion - Forge 1.20.1 port (unofficial)
==================================================

This is an unofficial port to Forge 1.20.1 of "Fishing Expansion" by lullaby
(https://github.com/lullaby6 / https://modrinth.com/datapack/ly-fishing-expansion),
originally a Minecraft 1.21.x datapack published under the
GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or (at your option) any later version.

Parts derived from the original: the idea and weights of mob fishing, the design of the
Sea Lure, Treasure Hunter, Hooked Strike, Ender Hook, Water Trail, Thundering and Dolphin's Grace
enchantments, the treasure table, the explorer maps, the shipwreck barrels and the boat structure.
New code in this port: the Java implementation of the enchantments and of mob fishing.
